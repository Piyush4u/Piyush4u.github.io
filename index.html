01 2016 – 2018 Emami Frank Ross Ltd.

It began with a mountain of paper.

Data Entry Operator

Invoices. ERP screens. The same twelve keystrokes, ten thousand times a week. Most people saw a workload. I started seeing a pattern, and every pattern is a program nobody has written yet.

What it taught me: accuracy is a habit long before it's a feature.

02 2018 – 2020 Centrum Insurance Brokers

Then I learned to read the numbers.

Quality Analyst · Reporting & KPIs

KPI tracking, trend reports, and feedback loops for sales teams. I learned that a dashboard isn't a picture. It's an argument. If it doesn't change a decision on Monday morning, it's decoration.

What it taught me: data only matters when it moves someone.

03 2021 – 2022 Apollo Pharmacy

Then I ran the floor.

Store In-Charge · Data-Driven Operations

Stock, staff, revenue, and someone walking in at 11 p.m. who needs medicine now. I ran the store on MIS reports. It taught me the rule I still design by: the real user is a tired human with no time for your clever tool.

What it taught me: build for the person at the counter, not for the slide deck.

04 2022 – Now Shyam Metalics & Energy Ltd.

Now I build the machines.

Automation & Analytics Developer

I joined as an MIS Executive and became the person the plant calls when a process hurts. I build SAP GUI bots that work the night shift, ML models that solve captchas so GST lookups run on their own, scrapers, Power BI dashboards and internal web apps.
